(https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf), fetched from apache.org. Changes from that text:

1. "The Apache Software Foundation" and "the Foundation" are replaced by "KRAVA IT SERVICES LTD".
2. Removed the instruction to email a signed PDF to secretary@apache.org. You sign this
   Agreement through the CLA Assistant check on your pull request instead.
3. Removed the contact form (full name, public name, address, country, e-mail, Apache ID,
   notify project); CLA Assistant records your GitHub account and the time of signing.
4. Removed the sentence on use "contrary to the public benefit or inconsistent with its
   nonprofit status and bylaws", which only applies to a nonprofit foundation.
5. Removed the signature/date line and the link to the ASF CLA privacy policy.

Sections 1–8 are otherwise unchanged.
