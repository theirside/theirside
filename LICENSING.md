## The SDK is MIT

The SDK you add to your app is MIT-licensed, so adding it to your app has no restrictions: use it in any product, open or closed, commercial or not.

## What you can do for free

Under the Fair Core License you can:

- read, run and modify the code;
- self-host the collector, dashboard and status page;
- use it in production for your own company;
- use it for non-commercial education and research, and in professional services you provide to someone who uses it under these terms.

## What you can't do

- Offer it, or something built from it, to others as a competing product or hosted service (the licence calls this a "Competing Use").
- Remove, disable or bypass the licence-key checks, or change the code behind them (including `ee/`) so paid features work without a valid key.

## Two years later it's Apache 2.0

Every release becomes available under the Apache License 2.0 two years after it is published, with no restrictions beyond Apache's. The newest code is under the FCL; older releases gradually become fully open source.

## Paid features and buying a licence

Paid features (see "Free vs paid" in the [README](README.md#free-vs-paid)) live in `ee/` and the same code is unlocked by a licence key. Paying customers get a licence key under separate commercial terms.
